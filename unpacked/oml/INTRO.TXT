<br>
Welcome, Hero!  You are one of the few humans who survived the journey to the harsh planet Morgana Prime, and thus have earned such a noble title.  You have pledged your loyalty to the Faction and it is for your Faction that you live, breathe, and kill.<br>

And kill you shall.  Your enemies are many.  Aliens lurk beneath the planet's surface and occasionally pour forth from their caverns to reclaim their planet, but far more dangerous are rival Factions.  Struggling for supremacy the Factions fight continuously for land and the precious and scarce resources they contain.<br>

Humans are never risked in the fighting, of course.  There are too few of us left for that and we have abandoned such barbarism long ago.  Machines of war, which we just call Units, do our fighting for us.
